# Email and Copywriting Playbook

## Installed Skills / Tools To Use

Use these for Job Hunt workflow:

- `resume-tailoring`: tailored CV for specific company, JD, or agency focus.
- `cv-creator`: polished ATS-friendly CV/PDF/DOCX/Markdown generation.
- `resume-ats-optimizer`: ATS check, keyword fit, formatting risk.
- `gmail`: draft/send/search sent mail; send only after explicit user approval.
- `google-sheets`: inspect/update tracker with exact ranges after send.
- `HumanCopy` connector: polish final email copy so it sounds human, direct, senior, and not AI-written.

Do not use generic marketing copy tone. Do not use buzzword-heavy cover-letter style.

## User Email Voice

Target voice:
- senior Product Designer
- direct and calm
- specific evidence, not claims
- confident but not loud
- short enough recruiter reads it fully
- not desperate, not over-explaining
- no corporate fluff
- no AI phrases

Avoid:
- "I am excited to apply"
- "I believe I would be a great fit"
- "passionate about creating user-centric solutions"
- "leveraging my expertise"
- "dynamic, results-driven"
- long autobiography
- inflated seniority
- vague "impactful designs"
- begging for opportunity

Prefer:
- "I’m reaching out because your team works across..."
- "My strongest fit is..."
- "At FuelBuddy, I worked on..."
- "I’m attaching a tailored CV focused on..."
- "If this maps to any current or upcoming product/design roles, I’d be glad to speak."

## Email Structure

Use 5 short parts:

1. Reason for writing.
2. One-line fit tied to company/agency sector.
3. Two proof points from real work.
4. Attached CV angle.
5. Low-pressure close.

## Agency Email Pattern

Subject:
`Product Designer - B2B SaaS, fintech and operations dashboards`

Body pattern:

```text
Hi {Name/Team},

I’m reaching out because {agency} works with {sector/company type}, and that overlaps with the product design work I’ve done across fintech, B2B operations, healthcare, and marketplaces.

My strongest fit is product design for complex workflows: at FuelBuddy, I worked on wallet/payment UX that reduced transaction errors by 38%, multi-wallet delegation, B2B business-account flows, live tracking, asset management, and operations dashboards. I’ve also worked on Meddo healthcare booking and doctor/back-office flows.

I’m attaching a tailored CV focused on {agency sector angle}. If you handle Product Designer, Senior Product Designer, UI/UX, SaaS, fintech, logistics, healthcare, or digital product roles, I’d be glad to speak.

Best,
Mohd Hayaat Ali
```

## Direct Employer Email Pattern

Subject:
`Product Designer - {specific role/team/company angle}`

Body pattern:

```text
Hi {Name/Team},

I’m reaching out about {role/team/company product area}. My background is product design for shipped mobile/web products, especially payment flows, B2B dashboards, operations tooling, and healthcare booking systems.

At FuelBuddy, I worked on wallet/payment UX that reduced transaction errors by 38%, multi-wallet delegation, business-account flows, asset management, and live operations dashboards. At Meddo, I worked across patient, doctor, and back-office flows, with booking improving from 71% to 83%.

I’m attaching a tailored CV for {role/company}. If this maps to the team’s current needs, I’d be glad to discuss.

Best,
Mohd Hayaat Ali
```

## Sector Angles

Fintech:
- FuelBuddy wallet/payment UX
- transaction errors down 38%
- multi-wallet users
- spend/credit limits
- trust, error recovery, account flows

Energy/logistics/oil:
- FuelBuddy B2B operations
- franchise dashboard
- live truck tracking
- asset/fleet/fuel workflows
- order delays and dispatch visibility

Healthcare:
- Meddo 2.0
- patient/doctor/back-office flows
- appointment booking 71% -> 83%
- doctor profile views +28%
- appointment requests +15%

Real estate/marketplace:
- search/catalog UX
- discovery flows
- consumer marketplace patterns
- conversion and listing detail pages

Agencies:
- state exact sector researched
- do not pretend there is a live role unless confirmed
- ask for relevant current/upcoming roles

## Quality Checks Before Showing User

Every email must pass:

- Under 170 words unless role needs context.
- Mentions specific company/agency reason.
- Has 1-2 hard proof points.
- Uses only truthful metrics.
- Says attached CV angle.
- No "excited", "passionate", "perfect fit", "user-centric".
- No generic paragraph that could be sent to anyone.
- No send without explicit approval.

## HumanCopy Settings

When using HumanCopy for final pass:

- channel: `email`
- directness: 85
- confidence: 80
- formality: 55
- warmth: 35
- brevity: 75
- humor: 0

Goal: sound like a senior designer who knows the work, not a candidate trying too hard.
